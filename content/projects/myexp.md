MyExp is a private expense tracker for iOS and Android built with Expo and React
Native. Type an expense in plain language and AI maps it to the right category.
On iOS, Siri can log it hands-free, while a share extension, home-screen widget,
and deep links make capture fast from anywhere.

Everything is stored locally in SQLite — no backend, no account. Trips split
costs across members, loans track money lent and borrowed, and PDF statements
are generated on-device. The native Siri, widget, and share-sheet integrations
are wired through custom Expo modules and config plugins.
